Matches your document’s style.

Paste new content into a resume or report and it comes out in that document’s fonts, colours, headings and bullets. In Google Docs it learns the design automatically whenever you copy something inside the document.

Never leaves you guessing.

After every paste, a small card says what happened: what it cleaned, why it left something alone, or how to fix it if an app refused the cleaned version. If in doubt, it steps aside and your normal paste happens.

Install it in a minute.

Magic Paste isn’t in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it installs as a developer extension.

  1. Download magic-paste-extension.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Refresh any open tabs, then copy something messy and paste it anywhere.

Private by design.

Everything runs on your computer. Nothing you copy or paste is sent anywhere or stored. Chrome will say the extension can read what you copy and paste on every site: that’s what lets it clean your pastes, and it’s all the access it uses.
